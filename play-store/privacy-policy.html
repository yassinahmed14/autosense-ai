Information used by the app

AutoSense AI lets you enter vehicle make/model/year, diagnostic codes, symptoms, safety selections and notes. When you request diagnostic guidance, these inputs and their source are transmitted to the configured diagnostic service. Code lookup transmits the requested code. Please do not enter personal details in notes.

Scanner and performance data

Bluetooth discovery accesses nearby scanner names, identifiers and service characteristics. Older Android versions require location permission for Bluetooth scanning; the app does not request GPS coordinates. Wi-Fi mode uses the adapter host and port you enter. Live vehicle readings, mass, run samples and session history are processed in device memory and are not automatically uploaded by the current code. When you copy a report or CSV, it is placed on your device clipboard.

Purpose, service providers and retention

Inputs are used to provide requested app functionality and preliminary rule-based guidance. The supplied backend processes requests in memory without a database. Hosting/network providers can receive IP addresses and connection metadata, and diagnostic codes in lookup URLs may appear in access logs. DEPLOYMENT_PROVIDER_AND_LOG_RETENTION_REQUIRED. Device session history ends when the app process ends; clipboard/exported copies follow the device or recipient's retention.

Accounts and advertising

The current version has no user accounts, ads, analytics or enabled cloud history. No advertising sale or sharing is implemented. Final hosting providers and any future SDKs must be disclosed here before release.

Subscriptions

When billing is enabled, Google Play handles payment. The app sends a purchase token to our configured backend to verify subscription status and expiry and acknowledge valid purchases with Google. AutoSense does not collect card details. Purchase-token retention, user binding and hosting practices must be completed before activation. Billing is disabled in development preview builds.
