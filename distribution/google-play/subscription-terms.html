1. Scope of Service & Safety Notice

AutoSense AI provides preliminary rule-based vehicle diagnostic guidance, generic SAE J2012 trouble-code definitions, live OBD-II telemetry display, and acceleration power estimates. Outputs are preliminary information only and never replace a qualified technician's inspection or establish that a vehicle is safe to drive. Do not interact with the app while driving. Compatible OBD-II hardware and vehicle protocol support are required for live scanner readings.

2. Freemium & AutoSense Pro Plans

  • Annual Plan: EGP 999.00/year (~$24.99/year, billed annually in full) after a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.
