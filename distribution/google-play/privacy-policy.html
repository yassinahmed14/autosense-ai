AutoSense AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Publisher: Youssef Ahmed • Support & Privacy Contact: youssefahmed4010@gmail.com

1. Information Used by the App

AutoSense AI lets you select a vehicle make/model/year and enter diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), symptoms, safety checklist selections, and optional diagnostic notes. When you request online diagnostic guidance, these inputs are sent over HTTPS to our stateless diagnostic service (https://autosense-api.vercel.app) or processed directly on your device via the built-in offline diagnostic engine. Please do not enter personal identifiers in diagnostic notes.

2. OBD-II Scanner & Performance Telemetry

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) discovery accesses nearby OBD-II scanner names, device identifiers, and serial GATT characteristics. On older Android versions, Android requires Location permission to scan for BLE devices; AutoSense AI does not collect or store GPS coordinates. Wi-Fi mode connects over local TCP to the adapter host and port you specify. Live vehicle readings (RPM, speed, coolant temperature, throttle position), vehicle mass, 10-second acceleration run samples, and session history are processed in device memory and are cleared when the app closes.

3. Purpose, Hosting & Data Retention

Diagnostic inputs are used solely to generate preliminary rule-based diagnostic guidance. The cloud API is hosted on Vercel and processes diagnostic requests in memory without a persistent user database. Standard serverless infrastructure access logs (such as IP address, request path, and timestamp) are retained temporarily by the hosting provider for security and operational reliability. On-device session history disappears when the app closes.

4. Accounts, Analytics & Advertising

AutoSense AI does not require user registration, contains no third-party advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.

5. Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

AutoSense AI offers a Freemium tier and optional AutoSense Pro subscriptions (Monthly at EGP 99.00/month and Annual at EGP 999.00/year, with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers). All payments are processed by Google Play. When Google Play billing verification is enabled, the app transmits the Google Play purchase token over HTTPS to our verification endpoint solely to validate active entitlement status and expiry with Google Play. AutoSense AI never receives or stores your credit card or payment instrument details.

6. Your Choices, Security & Contact

All cloud communication uses HTTPS encryption in transit. You may disconnect scanners, clear your in-memory session history, or revoke Bluetooth permissions in Android Settings at any time. For support, privacy questions, or data inquiries, contact youssefahmed4010@gmail.com.
